Aircraft & sky credits

The airborne showcase uses an exterior adapted from the FlightGear 787-family, under GNU GPL version 2. The original 787 model and its development are credited to Joshua W, Omega95, MSA-S23, Jonathan Redpath, Pilot2938, IskenderWang, Marsdolphin, Nicolás Castellán, Naviat and the other contributors listed in the accompanying Authors.md.

We converted the 787-9 and GEnx AC3D exterior to indexed glTF, merged static meshes, omitted landing gear, internal compressor stacks and duplicate fan blur discs, and encoded the original blank paint and engine textures as WebP. The personal livery, scroll camera choreography, lighting, sky composition and the engines' tail ends (a ring of turbine blades and a tempered-titanium nozzle finish with a faint ember inside, drawn at runtime in place of the omitted turbine stages) were added for this portfolio. The livery and registration are fictional.

Download corresponding aircraft source, converter, authors and GPL-2.0 license (ZIP). This includes the editable .blend and .ac assets and original texture files. The aircraft derivative remains GPL-2.0.

Sky & rendering

The moving sky photograph and transparent cloud artwork were generated for this site. Reflections use Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy (Pure Sky) by Greg Zaal and Jarod Guest, Poly Haven, CC0. The aircraft is rendered with Three.js (MIT). The livery roundel uses a Lucide plane glyph (ISC). Rendering license notices.

Optional wind and turbine ambience is synthesized locally with Web Audio. Motion can be disabled using the operating system’s reduced-motion setting.

The earlier Bay terrain study remains in the repository; its separate terrain and imagery credits are preserved.
