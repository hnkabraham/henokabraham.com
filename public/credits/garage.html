Garage model credits

The 2017 Shelby GT350 (non-R) in the Garage section represents my own car, adapted from the R-trim 2016 Ford Mustang Shelby GT350R Sketchfab model by Ddiaz Design, under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.

The 550 separate mesh parts in the original file were merged into one primitive per material, the body paint was repainted to match my own car — gray, with blue racing stripes, in place of the model's default scheme — the trim texture that rendered the front splitter and rocker skirts plain white was corrected, the large rear wing and its supports were removed, the detailed wheel texture was brightened toward neutral gunmetal while preserving its lettering and brake-disc detail, the roof panel — whose own UV layout painted almost the entire panel in the stripe color rather than tracking the body's gray-with-stripes pattern — was split out and repainted to match the hood and trunk, textures were re-encoded to WebP, and the result was Draco-compressed for the web. This derivative remains CC BY-NC-SA 4.0: non-commercial use, share-alike, with this attribution.

The remaining body and wheel geometry still come from the GT350R source, so this is a cosmetic approximation of my non-R GT350, not a scan of mine. A small lip spoiler is not separately modeled here — a nice enough stand-in until I get around to scanning the actual one. Reflections use the same daylight environment as the aircraft above; the car itself sits on a transparent background rather than a modeled road or sky.
