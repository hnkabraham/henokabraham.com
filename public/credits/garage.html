Garage model credits

The 2016 Shelby GT350R in the Garage section is adapted from 2016 Ford Mustang Shelby GT350R by Ddiaz Design, under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.

The 550 separate mesh parts in the original file were merged into one primitive per material, the body paint was repainted to match my own car — gray, with blue racing stripes, in place of the model's default scheme — the trim texture that rendered the front splitter, rocker skirts and rear wing plain white was corrected, textures were re-encoded to WebP, and the result was Draco-compressed for the web. This derivative remains CC BY-NC-SA 4.0: non-commercial use, share-alike, with this attribution.

This is a stock model of the car, not a scan of mine — a nice enough stand-in until I get around to scanning the actual one. Reflections use the same daylight environment as the aircraft above.
